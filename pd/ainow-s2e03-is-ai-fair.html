The news in 3 minutes

AI and fairness in the news

Stanford, 2026Feedback
Same essay, different feedback

When told a student's race, gender, language, or disability, AI models changed the feedback they gave on identical essays.

Researchers found "positive feedback bias" and "feedback withholding bias": more praise and less substantive critique for some groups.
The Learning Agency, 2025Grading
Can't tell good from bad

ChatGPT could mimic human essay scores but struggled to distinguish good writing from bad, and reproduced existing human biases.

In one analysis, GPT-4o scored essays almost a point lower than humans and penalized Asian American students more.
Jun 2026Hiring
Workday case proceeds

A federal judge refused to dismiss a lawsuit alleging Workday's AI screening tools discriminated by race, age, and disability.

More than 80% of U.S. employers use some form of AI screening in hiring.
Stanford, 2023Detection
Non-native writers flagged

Popular AI detectors misclassified many essays by non-native English writers as AI-generated.

Essays by U.S. eighth graders were mostly judged correctly, showing the bias falls on language learners.

Big idea

Where AI bias comes from

Bias can enter at three points, even when no one intends it.

Data

It learns from us

AI is trained on huge amounts of human writing, including our stereotypes.

Example: Feedback that addresses some students "more affectionately" mirrors patterns in human language.
Design

It notices surface style

AI can react to grammar, slang, or non-native phrasing instead of ideas.

Example: A 2026 study found informal language and non-native phrasing were graded lower even when the content was correct.
Deployment

Who it is used on

Bias matters most when AI makes decisions about people without a human check.

Example: A detector score used alone to accuse a multilingual student, with no conversation.

Takeaways

Three things to remember

AI learns our biases.

It is trained on human language, so it can repeat human stereotypes.

Bias can look like kindness.

More praise and less critique for some students is still unequal teaching.

Humans stay accountable.

Check, compare, and audit AI decisions, especially high-stakes ones.
