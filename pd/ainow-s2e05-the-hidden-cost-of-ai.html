AI and energy in 2026

IEA, 2026Global
Nearly double

Data center electricity demand grew 17% in 2025 and is projected to nearly double from about 485 TWh to 950 TWh by 2030.

That would be around 3% of global electricity demand.
IEA via Fortune, Apr 2026United States
About half

Data centers accounted for around half of all U.S. electricity demand growth last year.

Local opposition blocked or delayed at least 16 data centers worth a combined $64 billion.

The figures are a median, were not independently verified, and longer prompts, images, and video use more.

Big idea

Small per use, big in total

The environmental story of AI depends on what scale you look at.

Per prompt

Tiny

A single text prompt uses very little energy, and efficiency is improving fast.

Example: Google reported a 33-fold drop in energy per median prompt over one year.
Per day

Billions

Hundreds of millions of people sending prompts every day adds up quickly.

Example: When something feels cheap, we use more of it, so efficiency gains can be eaten up by growth.
Per place

Local

The costs land on specific towns: power grids, water supplies, and land.

Example: Residents near new data centers worry about electricity bills and water use.

Takeaways

Three things to remember

Small per prompt, big in total.

Each use is tiny; billions of uses strain grids and water.

The costs are local.

Communities near data centers carry much of the burden.

Use AI intentionally.

Choose AI when it adds real value, not by reflex.
