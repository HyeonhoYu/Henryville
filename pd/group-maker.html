How to use Group Maker

  1. Add your class. Type or paste names, one per line, or fill the list with numbers.
  2. Choose the groups. Set how many groups you want, or how many students go in each group.
  3. Pick a reveal. Shuffle shows every group at once. Spin the wheel picks one student at a time.
  4. Press Make groups. Use Shuffle again for a new mix, or Copy groups to paste the list anywhere.

Your class list stays saved in this browser, so it’s ready next time.

Version notes

Version 1.2

Hidden teacher settings open when you click the round logo three times, press and hold it for one second, or press Alt + Shift + S. There you can place students in groups and keep pairs apart, while the screen still shuffles and spins like normal.

When teacher settings are on, the small dot next to Group Maker at the bottom of the page turns darker. Open the settings before you connect to the projector.
